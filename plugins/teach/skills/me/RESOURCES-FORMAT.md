# RESOURCES.md

The trusted sources for this topic. Lessons draw their knowledge from here rather than from recall, and the communities listed here are where the user goes for judgement.

## TEMPLATE

```md
# {Topic} resources

## Knowledge

- [Book: The Science and Practice of Strength Training, Zatsiorsky and Kraemer](https://example.com)
  Year: 2006
  Foundational text on adaptation and programming. Reach for it on periodisation,
  recovery, intensity zones.
- [Article: How Much Should I Train, Greg Nuckols](https://example.com)
  Year: 2018
  Evidence review of volume landmarks. Reach for it on weekly set targets per muscle.

## Wisdom (communities)

- [r/weightroom](https://example.com)
  Moderated against bro-science, high signal. Reach for it on programme critique and
  plateaus.
- Local: Tuesday strength class, {gym}
  Reach for it on live coaching of the lifts themselves.

## Gaps
- Nothing solid found on {area the mission needs}. Search again before teaching it.

## Retired
- {source entry} - retired because {reason}; keep the link for old lesson references.
```

## RULES

- **High trust only.** Primary sources, recognised practitioners, peer-reviewed work, communities with real moderation. Marketing dressed as teaching stays out.
- **Accept two source forms.** A lesson may cite an entry in `RESOURCES.md` or a supplied theory file in `materials/`.
- **Annotate every entry.** One line: what it covers and when to reach for it. A bare link is dead weight in three months.
- **Date every knowledge source.** Add `Year: YYYY` for the publication or release year. Use the received year for a supplied material when no publication year exists.
- **Split knowledge from wisdom.** A source belongs in one group or the other, not both by default.
- **Name the gaps.** An area the mission needs and no good source covers goes under `## Gaps`. That list is what drives the next search.
- **Retire, do not delete.** A source that turned out shallow, wrong, or off-mission moves to `## Retired` with the reason, so old lesson references remain resolvable.
- **Record a refusal.** If the user does not want communities, write it here so no future session keeps suggesting them.
