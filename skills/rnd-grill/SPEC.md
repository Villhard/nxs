# rnd-grill

## Purpose

Investigate uncertainty and agree on a solution through proportionate evidence and meaningful choices. A useful result can remain in dialogue; save approved preparation artifacts directly when requested.

## Ownership

Own discussion, domain modeling, optional evidence and isolated-experiment guidance, and direct approved decision, feature-description, glossary/ADR and participant-questionnaire saving. Bundle the references and templates needed for independent installation. Project-spec reader rules remain owned by `dev-spec`.

## Boundaries

Explicit invocation or an instruction explicitly covering the work authorizes discussion, not unshown saves or implementation. No mandatory artifact chain, typed decision state machine, journal or coordination protocol applies. Preserve existing artifacts, identities and history; their labels are not independent authorization.

Only approved preparation/domain records and explicitly authorized isolated experiment files may be written. Product code/tests, implementation tickets, project specs, Git/index, settings and installations remain unchanged. Production transfer, reuse and deletion of prototype material each need separate authorization. Concrete environment or external actions also require action/target approval and native permissions.

## Contracts

- Resolve discoverable facts from evidence before asking for priorities. Present independent meaningful choices together, with real alternatives, consequences and recommendations; wait for the user's choices before deriving dependent questions. A clear request needs no forced interview.
- Keep agreement, proposals, assumptions, feedback and unanswered questions distinct. Resume settled conclusions without reopening them unless new contradictory evidence or the user's request affects them; preserving unfinished state never resolves its open choices.
- Use dependent-question mapping, primary-source research, opportunity surveys, selected-boundary design and isolated experiments only when the question calls for them. They are optional evidence methods, not hidden mandatory workflows.
- Direct saving shows content and destination, reuses only exact applicable approval, preserves source content and manual additions, and makes identical repeats a no-op. An unfinished result remains unfinished after approval to save it. Domain proposals are approved separately; feature descriptions are not project specs. Preparing a questionnaire does not authorize sending it.
- Sufficient agreed scope or an approved feature record can feed `dev-exec` directly; a decision/spec/ticket chain is not required. A handoff never starts another skill, implementation or a commit implicitly. Reconcile uncertain effects before continuing after interruption.
