# DISCUSSION

Read when discussing a decision or extracting/resuming its saved state. These shared rules have one logical owner, rnd-grill; installed consumers use their own identical copy.

## EVIDENCE

Resolve discoverable facts yourself from relevant code, callers, tests and documents before asking the user. State what the evidence establishes, what is assumed and what remains unverified. Scale investigation to uncertainty and consequences. An unavailable source is missing evidence, not confirmation or absence.

Read supplied decisions and applicable glossary/ADRs first. Retain their scope, exclusions, reasons, feedback and unanswered questions. The existence of a document or a ready label does not establish approval. A clear request with no material alternatives needs no forced interview.

## ROUNDS

The frontier contains the meaningful decisions whose prerequisites are settled. Ask independent frontier questions together, within the client's question limits; dependent questions wait for the preceding answers. Show concrete alternatives with observable consequences and a reasoned recommendation. Use an open answer when choices would invent false alternatives.

Ordinary discussion waits for the user's choice. Recompute the frontier after each answer. Evidence resolves factual questions; personal priorities and changes to agreed decisions require the user's answer. An internal detail with equivalent outcomes is a technical choice, not an artificial user question.

Pressure-test significant conclusions with concrete scenarios, assumptions, failure paths and observable signals for reconsideration. Finish when every material decision is agreed or explicitly left open. A limit on investigation never converts an unresolved question into agreement.

## SAVE AND RESUME

Keep agreed conclusions distinct from proposals, assumptions and open questions. Explicitly requested unfinished state may be saved after its proposed content is shown and approved; approval to preserve uncertainty does not answer it. Include user feedback and its disposition.

Resume from saved state and check relevant current facts. Reopen an agreed conclusion only on new contradictory evidence or the user's request; explain the affected conclusion and evidence. Preserve the rest. Never infer saving, spec creation, tickets or implementation from agreement alone. HTML approval alone is neither a save request nor permission to start another stage.
